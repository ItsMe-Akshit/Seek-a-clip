The difference

The parts of clipping that eat your week.

Not a highlight generator that guesses. A clipping workflow that watches, judges, cuts, captions and posts — then shows you exactly what it did.

Two judges. One decision.

One AI reads only what was said. Another sees only how loud the room got and how busy chat was. A clip is made when both agree, so you get moments, not filler.

What does Seek-a-clip actually do?
Paste a YouTube, Twitch or Kick link, or upload a video. Seek-a-clip finds the strongest moments, cuts them into vertical 9:16 clips with word-by-word captions, and gives each one a hook, title, description and hashtags.
Can it clip while a stream is still live?
Yes. Add a streamer once and Seek-a-clip can record their live stream by itself, check it as often as every minute, and have clips ready before the stream ends. You can also record a stream on demand.
Do I need my own AI key?
No. AI is included on this server. If you prefer to use your own Gemini, OpenAI or compatible key, you can connect it in Settings, AI. Without any key the app still works, picking loud moments by reaction instead.
Which videos work best?
Anything with people talking and reacting: live streams, gaming VODs, podcasts, interviews. Links can come from YouTube, Twitch or Kick, or you can upload a file. Captions are written from English speech.
How long are the clips, and what shape?
Vertical 9:16, the shape Shorts, Reels and TikTok use. They run 15 to 60 seconds by default and you can set your own range. Choose a blurred fill, a zoomed frame or a centre crop.
Can I change a clip afterwards?
Yes. Open any clip to trim the start and end, rewrite the hook and title, or switch caption style and layout, then remake it. The original recording is kept, so you can always pull another moment.
Can I clip any streamer?
Seek-a-clip is a tool. You are responsible for having the right to clip and post a creator's content; most clipping programs ask you to follow the creator's own rules.
